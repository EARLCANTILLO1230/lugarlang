Key Transport Policies

Tabang Pangbyahe Program
A DOTr‑Cebu Provincial Government initiative that subsidises operators on low‑demand routes to prevent commuter stranding. Vehicles are required to have GPS tracking linked to LGU command centres.

PUV Modernisation
Only consolidated cooperatives are issued franchises. Traditional jeepneys are allowed only if part of a cooperative actively transitioning to Euro‑4 or electric fleets.
